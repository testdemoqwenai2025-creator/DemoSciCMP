Quality Control & Homogenization

Detect outliers, correct for station moves, apply homogenization algorithms for consistent time series.

Trend Analysis

# Calculate trends from scipy import stats slope, intercept, r_value, p_value, std_err = stats.linregress( range(len(temp_anomaly)), temp_anomaly.values.flatten()) print(f'Trend: {slope*10:.2f}°C/decade, p={p_value:.4f}')

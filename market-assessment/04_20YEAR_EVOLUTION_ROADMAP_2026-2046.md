## Executive Summary

This roadmap charts SciCMP's evolution from a **2026 scientific computing startup** to a **2046 foundational platform** that serves as the operating system for human scientific discovery. The journey spans five distinct eras, each characterized by dominant technology paradigms, market conditions, and platform capabilities.

**Core Thesis:** Scientific discovery is undergoing a fundamental transformation from **human-centric, intuition-driven research** to **human-AI collaborative, computation-enabled exploration**. SciCMP is positioned to be the infrastructure layer upon which this transformation occurs—adapting continuously across technology waves while maintaining core values of accessibility, reproducibility, and scientific rigor.

**Key Evolution Principles:**
1. **Abstract Today, Concrete Tomorrow:** Build interfaces that can absorb new compute substrates
2. **Human-in-the-Loop Forever:** Augment scientists, never replace their judgment
3. **Open by Default:** Ecosystem effects create durable competitive advantage
4. **Progressive Enhancement:** Work everywhere, excel where capable
5. **Temporal Longevity:** Design decisions must survive two decades of change

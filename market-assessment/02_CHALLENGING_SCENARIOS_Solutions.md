## Executive Summary

Strategic scenario planning is essential for startup survival and long-term success. This document presents **seven challenging scenarios** that SciCMP may encounter on its path from seed-stage startup to enduring scientific platform. Each scenario includes detailed context, impact analysis, strategic response options, and recommended actions.

#### Option A: Differentiation Defense (Recommended Primary)
**Strategy:** Double down on areas where Big Tech cannot or will not compete


**Strategy:** Position as DeepLab enhancement layer rather than competitor

**Approach:**
- Build integrations with DeepLab API
- Offer "DeepLab + SciCMP Pro" bundled experience
- Target users who outgrow DeepLab's capabilities
- Become the "power user" upgrade path

**Strategy:** Abandon broad market, focus on defensible niche

**Potential Niches:**
- Classified/defense research (can't use Google)
- Highly regulated industries (pharma audit requirements)
- Specific geographic markets (China, EU data residency)

**Strategy:** Controlled rewrite of core systems while maintaining service

**Strategy:** Fix only what's broken, defer fundamental changes

**Approach:**

- Each fix becomes harder due to system fragility

**Approach:**

**Risks:**
- Very high risk of failure
- Takes 18-24 months minimum
- Diverts all engineering from customer-facing work
- May arrive to find market has moved on

## Scenario 4: The Founder Conflict Crisis

### Context

**Date:** Month 15 of operations (Q3 2027)  
**Trigger Event:** Fundamental disagreement emerges between co-founders on company direction, pace, or roles. One founder may want to exit or change involvement level.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                FOUNDER CONFLICT CRISIS SCENARIO                  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                  │
│  CONFLICT MANIFESTATIONS:                                       │
│  • Public disagreements in team meetings                        │
│  • Decisions made then undermined privately                     │
│  • Communication through third parties rather than directly     │
│  • One founder disengaging (remote, absent, quiet quitting)     │
│                                                                  │
│  COMMON DISAGREEMENT TOPICS:                                    │
│  • Vision: Enterprise focus vs. academic/community focus         │
│  • Pace: Aggressive growth vs. sustainable building             │
│  • Roles: Who leads product, technology, business                │
│  • Equity: Perception of unfair contribution vs. reward         │
│  • Exit: One founder wants faster exit (acquisition) vs. build  │
│                                                                  │
│  BUSINESS RISKS:                                                │
│  • Decision paralysis at leadership level                       │
│  • Team picks sides, culture deteriorates                       │

│  • Worst case: Company dissolution, IP disputes                 │
│                                                                  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Impact Assessment

| Dimension | Severity | If Unresolved |
|-----------|----------|---------------|
| Decision Making | Critical | Paralysis |
| Company Culture | Severe | Toxic environment |
| Investor Confidence | High | Funding at risk |
| Execution Velocity | High | Stalled progress |
| Personal Relationships | Severe | Permanent damage |

### Strategic Response Options

#### Option A: Structured Mediation (Recommended First Step)

**Process Framework:**

```yaml
Mediation_Process:
  Phase_1_Acknowledgment:
    timeline: Week 1
    actions:
      - Acknowledge conflict exists (don't minimize)
      - Agree to resolve before it worsens
      - Commit to process, not just outcome
    
  Phase_2_Neutral_Facilitation:
    timeline: Weeks 2-4
    actions:
      - Engage professional mediator (experienced with startups)
      - Each founder prepares position independently
      - Facilitated discussion sessions (3-4 meetings)
      - Document agreements and disagreements
    
  Phase_3_Root_Cause_Analysis:
    timeline: Weeks 5-6
    exploration_areas:
      - Is this about vision or execution?
      - Are there personal/professional boundary issues?
      - Does equity allocation need adjustment?
      - Are roles clear and appropriate for skills?
    
  Phase_4_Resolution_Options:
    possible_outcomes:
      - Realigned roles and responsibilities
      - Adjusted decision rights (areas of autonomy)
      - Equity refresh (vesting acceleration, new grants)
      - Buyout of one founder
      - Amicable separation with ongoing advisor role
```

**Founder Agreement Checklist for Prevention:**

| Topic | Should Be Documented |
|-------|---------------------|
| Roles & Responsibilities | Explicit job descriptions |
| Decision Rights | What decisions require consensus vs. unilateral |
| Vesting | Standard 4-year with 1-year cliff |
| Departure Triggers | What happens if founder leaves (good/bad leaver) |
| Dispute Resolution | Mediation then arbitration process |
| Non-compete/non-solicit | Reasonable limitations |

#### Option B: Role Restructuring

**Strategy:** Redefine founder roles to match actual interests and skills

**Example Restructuring:**

| Founder | Before | After |
|---------|--------|-------|
| CEO/Co-founder | Everything (overloaded) | External facing only (fundraising, partnerships) |
| CTO/Co-founder | Everything technical | Product vision + architecture (less management) |
| **New Hire** | N/A | COO/VP Operations (internal operations) |

**Benefits:**
- Clearer ownership reduces friction
- Each founder plays to strengths
- Removes direct competition for decisions

#### Option C: Founder Departure

**Strategy:** One founder exits, company continues

**Structuring a Fair Departure:**

```yaml
Departure_Terms_Good_Leaver:
  equity_treatment: 
    vested: Keep vested shares
    unvested: Extended exercise window (3-10 years)
  role_transition: 
    title: "Founder Emeritus" or Advisory Board seat
    compensation: 
      cash: Severance package (if applicable)
      equity: Maybe small additional grant for goodwill
  
Departure_Terms_Bad_Leaver:
  equity_treatment:
    vested: Company repurchase right (often at cost)
    unvested: Forfeited immediately
  restrictions:
    non_compete: Enforce standard terms
    non_disparagement: Mutual obligation
```

### Recommended Action Plan

**Prevention > Cure:**

**Before Conflict Arises (Do Now):**
1. Sign comprehensive Founders Agreement
2. Establish regular "state of the union" check-ins
3. Define explicit decision rights matrix
4. Create conflict escalation protocol
5. Engage board member or advisor as neutral party

**When Conflict Emerges:**
1. Address within 48 hours (don't let fester)
2. Move conversation out of team view (private setting)
3. Consider professional mediation early
4. Prioritize company health over ego
5. Document everything in writing

## INVESTOR FAQ QUICK REFERENCE

### Common Questions

**Q: Why won't Google/Microsoft just build this?**
A: They lack domain expertise; their incentives favor broad platforms over deep scientific capability. We're 3 years ahead on specialized workflows.

**Q: How do you compete with free academic software?**
A: Free software requires expertise to use. We make advanced computation accessible to non-experts—the much larger market.

**Q: What's your moat against open-source alternatives?**
A: We embrace open-source (open-core model). Our moat is ease of use, integration, and ecosystem—not source code secrecy.

**Q: When will you be profitable?**
A: Path to profitability by Year 4-5 (2030), with option to invest longer for faster growth.

**Q: What's your exit strategy?**
A: Build a durable, independent company. Potential exits at $1B+ valuation in 7-10 years if desired.
